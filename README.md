# Data Analytics & Business Analysis Portfolio
This repository contains a collection of data analytics projects leveraging Python, SQL, Power BI, and Looker Studio, with a strong focus on applying both Business Analysis and Data Analytics practices. These projects highlight my ability to process raw data, perform exploratory analysis, and translate insights into actionable recommendations that support business decision-making.

## Projects
### 1. Banking_EDA.ipynb
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on banking data to identify customer behavior and transaction trends.

### 2. CSI_INACA_Data_Preparation.ipynb
Data preparation for Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) survey of INACA, supporting service quality evaluation in the Indonesian aviation sector.

### 3. Fashion_Retail_Sales_Analytics.ipynb
Sales analysis in the fashion retail industry to identify purchasing patterns, seasonal trends, and business strategy insights.

### 4. Leads_Analytics_EDA.ipynb
Lead journey and sales interaction analytics to optimize the sales funnel and increase conversion opportunities.

### 5. Regional_Sales_Analytics.ipynb
Regional sales performance analysis to map business growth and provide distribution strategy recommendations.

### 6. SQL_Script_for_Leads_Analytics.sql
SQL scripts used for data cleaning, wrangling, and integration before advanced analytics in Python.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- SQL (data cleaning & wrangling)
- Power BI (interactive dashboards & advanced visualization)
- Google Looker Studio (business reporting & data storytelling)

## Relevance to Business Analyst Role
This portfolio demonstrates how I combine Business Analysis with Data Analytics by:
1. Structuring raw data into accessible, business-ready information.
2. Conducting exploratory and diagnostic analysis to identify key insights.
3. Designing dashboards and reports that support stakeholder decision-making.
4. Bridging the gap between business requirements and data-driven solutions.
